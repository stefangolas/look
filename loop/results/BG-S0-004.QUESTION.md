Those two additions drive two housekeeping counters that the packet also
requires to pass, and neither fix is writable:

1. **`tests/tolerance_stepio.rs::every_migrated_stepio_site_is_marked`**
   (included in the done-when command `cargo test -p truck-stepio --lib
   --tests --no-fail-fast`). It counts predicate lines and `// BG-TOL-001:`

   predicate lines force new markers (per-file balance), so the total becomes
   **21**, and the assertion fails: `expected 19 migrated sites, found 21
   markers`. Fixing it means editing `tests/tolerance_stepio.rs`, which is not
   in `write_allow`.

2. **`scripts/kernel-gates.sh` GATE-4** (the packet says to run it and that V4
   runs it). It counts `unscaled_legacy(` call sites at HEAD against
   `scripts/unscaled_legacy_ceiling.txt`, which is currently **109** (the tree
   is exactly at the ceiling). The new call makes it **110**, so GATE-4 fails:
   `110 unscaled_legacy() call sites, ceiling is 109`. Fixing it means raising
   the ceiling file, which is also not in `write_allow`.

I did not edit either file (both are outside `write_allow`, and the packet
forbids editing files outside it). The two "fixes" needed to turn this into a
green DONE are therefore:

- `tests/tolerance_stepio.rs`: `assert_eq!(markers, 19, ...)` → 21, or the
  count made dynamic; and
- `scripts/unscaled_legacy_ceiling.txt`: `109` → `110` (with the loop's usual
  ceiling-bump bookkeeping).
